Your data, under your control

Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

My Finances («Мои финансы») is an Android app for keeping personal financial records locally. The developer does not receive your financial database or AI messages on a server operated by the developer. Connecting Google Drive for backups is a separate, optional feature.

1. Data on your device

The app processes the accounts, opening balances, income, expenses, transfers, categories, dates, transaction names and comments you enter. This data and your financial settings are stored in a local database and used to show your history, calculate balances and produce charts and totals.

Your assistant chat history, its replies and proposed change cards are stored on your device. Everyday financial tracking does not require a separate account with the developer.

2. Local AI and model downloads

You can choose to download an AI model separately from Hugging Face. Once downloaded, requests are processed on your device: messages and financial records are not sent to the developer or to a cloud service for generating replies. Changes proposed by the assistant require your confirmation.

An internet connection is needed to download the model file. The download service may receive technical request information, including your IP address, under its privacy policy. The download request does not include your financial database or chat history.

3. Optional Google Drive backups

When you select “Connect Google Drive”, Google asks for consent to the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope. This lets the app work with its own backups in your Drive’s hidden app folder. The app does not request full access to your other Drive files.

To show the selected connection and associate backups with it, the app obtains the account’s email address and technical Drive account identifier. These details and the backup status are stored on your device. Access is granted through Google Play services; the app does not ask for your Google password.

An archive of your financial database and the metadata needed to validate and restore it are uploaded to your Google account. The archive does not include chat history, AI model weights or Google authorization data. Backups are not sent to the developer’s Drive or server.

Access is used to create, find, read, restore and delete the app’s backups. After a successful upload, the app attempts to keep the five most recent backups by deleting older ones. Google Drive and Google Play services are governed by Google’s privacy policy.

4. Managing access and deleting data

You can turn off automatic backups or disconnect Google Drive in your profile. When you disconnect, the app stops backups and requests that access be revoked. You can also revoke permission in your Google Account connection settings.

Turning off backups, disconnecting Drive or uninstalling the app does not automatically delete existing cloud backups. You can manage hidden app data in Google Drive’s settings for connected apps. Clearing the app’s data through Android settings deletes local records; remotely stored cloud backups and exported files are managed separately.

You can clear the chat history in the app, delete a downloaded model, export the database to a file or restore a selected backup. Once confirmed, a restore replaces your current financial data with the backup’s contents.

5. Export and support

When exporting data, you choose where to store the archive and how to share it. The archive contains financial records; access depends on the storage location and recipients you choose.

If you contact the developer by email, the developer receives the sender’s address and the information you include in your message or attachments, and uses it to respond to your request. You do not need to send your financial database to use the app normally.

6. Advertising and data use

The current version does not use advertising SDKs or third-party tools that track your behavior in the app. The developer does not use financial records or Google data for advertising, selling data or training AI models. This website has no financial data upload forms or analytics trackers operated by us.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical visit data, including IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details.

7. Updates and contact

If data handling changes, this policy will be updated with a new revision date. For questions about the app, data handling or support, contact the developer at make.lz.engineering@gmail.com.
