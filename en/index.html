You control your records

A local database.
Backups when you choose.

On your phone

Accounts, transactions, categories and AI chat history are stored locally. Your financial database and AI messages are not sent to a server operated by the developer.

In your Google Drive

If you connect Google Drive, the app can save and restore backups of your financial database in its hidden app folder in your account. It requests drive.appdata access, rather than access to all your Drive files.

Under your control

Connecting Drive is optional. In your profile, you can manage automatic backups, disconnect Drive, export your data to a file or choose a backup to restore.
