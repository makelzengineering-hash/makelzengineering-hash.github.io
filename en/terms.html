Using the app

Terms of use

Last updated: 24 October 2026

These terms describe the use of the My Finances Android app and its optional features. You can contact the developer at make.lz.engineering@gmail.com.

1. Purpose of the app

The app is intended for keeping personal financial records: accounts, income, expenses, transfers, categories and analysis of the data you enter. Recording a transfer between accounts changes records within the app and does not execute a bank payment. The app does not connect to a bank to manage real money.

2. Your records and confirmations

You decide what data to enter, change, export or delete. Totals and charts depend on the completeness and accuracy of those records. Before confirming transactions, bulk moves or database restores, check the amounts, accounts, categories, dates and scope of changes shown.

The app does not replace a bank statement or mandatory accounting reports. Its calculations are not a promise of future income and are not intended for making investment decisions.

3. AI assistant

The assistant uses a separately downloaded local model and may misunderstand text or make mistakes when explaining a result. Financial data changes are shown for your confirmation. Review the change card before saving.

AI availability and speed depend on your Android device’s compatibility, available memory, storage for the model and device conditions. Downloading a model requires internet access; after installation, requests are processed locally.

4. Google Drive and backups

Cloud backups are optional and use your Google account. They require an internet connection, available Drive storage, Google Play services and valid Google authorization. Background execution depends on Android restrictions.

Check the date of your last successful backup. Connecting Drive alone does not confirm a successful upload. You can also export the database to a file. Once you confirm a restore, the selected backup replaces the current financial database.

You can turn off backups or disconnect Google Drive. Details about backup contents, access and deletion are provided in the privacy policy.

5. Use and support

Use the app for data you are authorized to manage. Protect access to your phone, Google account and exported archives. When contacting support, share only the information needed to describe the issue.

Feature descriptions on this website reflect the current state of the app; features and the interface may change with updates. If paid features are offered, their terms and price must be shown before purchase. These pages do not themselves create a purchase or replace the distribution store’s terms.

6. Privacy and changes to these terms

Data handling is described in the privacy policy. New versions of these terms will be published here with an updated date. These terms do not limit any user rights that cannot be excluded under applicable law.

For questions about using the app or your data, contact make.lz.engineering@gmail.com.
